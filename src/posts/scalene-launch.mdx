I've been a long-time Vercel user. It's where I host [this blog](/blog/welcome-to-my-new-blog), as well as the large majority
of my other projects, like [hypothesis.sh](blog/claude-code-developer-tools). One thing I was really
missing was a native app for iPhone and iPad that not only allowed me to look up my deployments and analytics, but also
view live deployment statuses and other realtime metrics.

Today, after a few months of work, I'm happy to announce that I solved my own problem (and hopefully one of yours too!), and launched [Scalene](https://scalene.app) for iOS and iPad!

## Scalene for Vercel

Scalene has a bunch of features for viewing analytics and usage accross your projects, laid out to be glanceable whether you have one or many.

![](scalene-screens-shadow.png?hideShadow)

There is also deep integration with iOS to keep you informed (my favorite part): Live activities and push notifications for every deployment, homescreen widgets
with deployment status, traffic analytics, and performance insights, as well as a lockscreen widget!

These live activities and push notifications
work through a relay. When you first connect the app, a webhook is installed into your account, secured with a token, pointing at the Scalene relay, and any additional users or devices
that connect to the same account or team share the single webhook. The relay handles fanning out push notifications and live activities to authenticated devices.

![](scalene-widgets.png?hideShadow)

Scalene lets your view each build log, and manage redeploying and promoting builds. Notifications can be configured on a per-project
basis depending on your desire, and are device-specific, so one device can be noisier than another. There's even an Apple Watch companion too!

![](scalene-screens-2.png?hideShadow)

The app is built for iPad from the start, and I have in-progress a dedicated Mac app. In the meantime, the iPad app running on Apple Silicon Macs works quite well.

![](scalene-ipad.png?hideShadow)

I have an Android version in development as well that I expect will hit the Play Store in the coming weeks. I've been testing it on my Pixel 11 for the past week or so
and it's been working really nicely, though the integration surfaces are a little different than iOS.
